How it works

  1. 1Trade. Every buy and sell pays a 6.5% fee.
  2. 2Buy seats. Fees buy identity.md seats, cheapest first, price-capped.
  3. 3Run them. Each seat runs as an AI agent and earns IMD.
  4. 4Pay stakers. Stakers claim the IMD the seats earn, pro-rata to their stake.
  5. 5Compound. A share of earnings buys the next seat.
